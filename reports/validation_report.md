# FraudShield validation report

Status: draft. Sections marked TODO are completed before publishing.

## 1. Intended use and scope
The model ranks online bank-account applications so analysts review the highest-risk ones first, within a fixed review capacity. It supports human review and makes no automatic accept or reject decision. It was built on a synthetic benchmark as a methodology exercise and must not be used for real fraud decisions (the dataset authors say the same).

## 2. Data
- BAF Base: 1,000,000 synthetic account applications, 32 columns, months 0-7, fraud rate 1.10%. Generated by a CTGAN trained on noised real data. License CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; the data is not in this repository.
- Missing values are coded as -1 in five columns and as negatives in intended_balcon_amount (confirmed by the datasheet). They are converted to NaN with was-missing flags; missingness itself carries signal (for example, fraud rate 1.42% when prev_address_months_count is missing vs 0.31% otherwise).
- Labels exist only for applications the bank accepted; there are none for pre-rejected applicants.
- Details: `data_audit.md`.

## 3. Evaluation protocol
Time-based split: train months 0-4, validation month 5, test months 6-7. The final model was retrained on months 0-5 and evaluated once on the test months. Primary metrics: PR-AUC, recall at fixed false-positive rate, recall at a fixed review budget, with bootstrap 95% intervals. Protocol and amendments: `evaluation_protocol.md`
- Provenance: the test months were evaluated once; all model, feature and threshold decisions were committed before the results (see the "Test-set evaluation" amendment in `evaluation_protocol.md`, and commit 3616fdd)..

## 4. Model comparison (validation month, models trained on months 0-4)
| Model | PR-AUC | Recall at 5% FPR |
|---|---|---|
| Dummy (prior) | 0.012 | 0.045 |
| Red-flag rules (no age) | 0.024 (0.022-0.026) | 0.158 (0.139-0.177) |
| Logistic regression (no age) | 0.161 (0.140-0.184) | 0.510 (0.485-0.537) |
| Gradient-boosted trees (no age) | 0.186 (0.163-0.209) | 0.532 (0.506-0.557) |

Paired difference, boosted trees minus logistic regression: PR-AUC +0.025 (0.016 to 0.037); recall at 5% FPR +0.022 (0.005 to 0.039). Selected: gradient-boosted trees. Reasoning: `model_selection.md`.

## 5. Leakage and robustness checks
- Removing credit_risk_score changed validation PR-AUC from 0.184 to 0.182 and recall at 5% FPR from 0.530 to 0.529: the model does not simply reproduce the bank's own score.
- Adding days_since_request gave PR-AUC 0.186 and recall 0.528: no sign of leakage and no benefit. It stays excluded because the datasheet does not say when it is measured.
- customer_age gave no accuracy gain in either model and is excluded.
- Limit: one-column-at-a-time checks cannot rule out leakage spread across several columns.

## 6. Threshold and cost analysis (validation month, hypothetical costs)
Policy: review the top X% of applications by score. A missed fraud costs R times a review (R unknown; 10, 50 and 200 tested).
| R | Cost-minimising review share | Fraud caught | Precision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 1.8% | 32% | 21% |
| 50 | 14.6% | 75% | 6% |
| 200 | 29.2% (edge of range) | 89% | 3.6% |

No single threshold is optimal without a justified cost assumption. Operating point chosen: top 5%. Scores under-predict fraud in the highest deciles, so they are used for ranking only and not shown as probabilities. Details: `threshold_analysis.md`.

## 7. Fairness (age 50+ vs under 50, 5% review budget)
| Split | Group | Fraud rate | Flagged share | False-positive rate | Recall | Precision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Validation | Under 50 | 0.95% | 4.0% | 3.64% | 44.5% | - |
| Validation | 50+ | 2.27% | 9.6% | 8.37% | 62.8% | - |
| Test | Under 50 | 1.10% | 4.04% | 3.56% | 46.5% | 12.7% |
| Test | 50+ | 2.93% | 9.88% | 8.38% | 59.8% | 17.7% |

Excluding age does not remove the disparity: honest applicants aged 50+ are flagged about 2.3 times as often, tracking the difference in base fraud rates. With different base rates, equal false-positive rates and equal flag reliability cannot both hold. This project reports the gap and does not use group-specific cutoffs, which would use age explicitly in the decision. TODO: test whether removing proxy features narrows the gap.

## 11. Limitations
- Synthetic benchmark data; month-level fraud prevalence was set by the dataset's sampling, so stability here does not prove real-world stability.
- Costs are hypothetical; no savings are claimed.
- Intervals reflect sampling variation only, not fitting randomness.
- One model family comparison, one test period.
